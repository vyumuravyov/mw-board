Rules & mappings
E rules: 57=R, 48/49=D, 55=H, 58=TRI, blank/unknown=?. Driver column uses first name with normal capitalization; Oleg keeps the surname initial. BACK BY remains uppercase. Future BACK BY dates are kept (example: NASIR BACK BY 10.09). Expired BACK BY dates are ignored. Exactly one blank row is inserted between state groups (and between Chicago-area IL and Downstate IL). Yellow cells need a quick manual check. F and G are never included.
